Welcome to EEG Biophysics

This simulator visualizes the micro-scale electrical phenomena that build up the macro-scale EEG waveforms measured by clinical and scientific systems.

Click on the Head Cross-Section layers or use the Guided Physics Concepts to begin investigating how electrical currents propagate through the skull.

Key Biophysics Fact

Individual action potentials (spikes) do not generate EEG signals. Instead, EEG is formed by the spatial summation of slower synaptic potentials (EPSPs and IPSPs).

Based on Freeman (2004a, b) biophysical models of cortical dipoles.
