Interactive Science Guide

Step 1

Neurovascular Coupling

Clicking "Trigger Neural Activation" stimulates neurons. This triggers localized oxygen consumption and triggers a rapid increase of regional cerebral blood flow.

Step 2

The Near-Infrared Window

Living tissues are transparent to NIR light (650-900nm), allowing photons to penetrate scalp, skull, and CSF, reaching up to 1-2cm into cerebral cortex.

Step 3

Modified Beer-Lambert Law

Because light scatters in a banana-shaped path, the modified Beer-Lambert Law calculates relative concentration changes of O₂Hb and HHb based on optical density changes.
