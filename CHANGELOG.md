# Changelog

All notable changes to Project Viz are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed

- Partitioning Sum of Squares fits its tree on a phone. The chart was centered at a fixed width, 680px, or 1180px when a design had ten or more leaves, so a narrow panel clipped the left side and scrolled the right. Under the phone shell (736px) that minimum is gone. A short tree stays horizontal and uses the panel width. A tree whose boxes would be narrower than about 128px stacks: each box uses the panel, children sit under the parent, and the page is the scroller. The cards under the tree shrink with the panel, so a long F table does not widen the page. Wider windows keep the horizontal chart. The sums of squares, Teach and Learn, and the citation version are unchanged.

- Guided Tracks on a phone lines up the Back, Previous, and Next labels. Those three controls already share one line height, one padding, and a 44px box. Back is a flex box, so its label sat on the top of that box, while Previous and Next stayed ordinary buttons and centered their labels. The same centering now applies to all three, including when Previous or Next is unavailable. The pane title stays hidden, Teach and Learn are unchanged, and a wider screen is unchanged. The citation version is unchanged.

- Teach and Learn on a phone keeps the selected name filled. A tap left `:hover` on the button, and the selected name’s hover rule then painted that name white, so the control no longer showed which mode was on. Hover colors now apply only for a fine pointer that can hover. The fill still follows the mode, including after a tap, on reload, and with `?teach=1` or `?learn=1`. On a phone the demonstration title in the Catalog and Guided Tracks bar is hidden. That heading was the only item that could shrink, so a narrow row left a character or nothing. The title inside the demonstration stays. Wider screens still show the bar title. Back and Teach / Learn stay usable. The choice still only shows or hides regions. The citation version is unchanged.

- Geometric ANOVA Regression data points stay visible on the white 3D scene. Each point was a group-colored sphere inside a larger opaque white sphere, and that white shell was the surface the camera saw, so the points read as white on the white background. The shell is now an ink rim drawn from the inside of a slightly larger sphere, and the group color is the surface. The scene also writes those hex colors as stored. An sRGB framebuffer had been encoding them a second time, so the hidden fill would still have been a pale tint of Group A blue and Group B teal. Group A stays blue, Group B stays teal, and residual lines stay residual rose. The 2D cross-section markers already use those fills with a white stroke on the colored mark, and they were left as they are. Teach, Learn, the five designs, the camera presets, and the citation version are unchanged.

- Fullscreen in a home-screen web app. iPadOS 26 opens a site saved with Share → Add to Home Screen, or Add to Dock, as a web app even when the site has no manifest. In that window the Fullscreen API is missing or the request is refused, so the Fullscreen button on Catalog and Guided Tracks did nothing. The button still calls the browser Fullscreen API on the demo pane when that API is available, including in iPad Safari. When this page is a home-screen web app and the request cannot start, the same button hides the top bar, the rail, and the footer so the open demonstration fills the window. The demonstration’s iframe keeps filling the stage, so the pane does not gain a scrollbar. Exit, Back, and Escape bring the chrome back. Teach and Learn are unchanged. The citation version is unchanged.

- Catalog no longer stays on “Loading catalog…”. The category list paints as soon as `demos.json` arrives, including when the shell script is late or missing. The shell still attaches navigation and the open demonstration when it loads. A hung or failed catalog fetch, or a catalog script that never runs, shows “The catalog could not be loaded.” and a Retry control. Guided Tracks uses the same timeout, error, and Retry path, so it does not stay on “Loading tracks…”. The citation version is unchanged.

- Chi-Square Distribution panel 1 keeps its squared-normal notes above the curve. “Values in [-1, 1] shrink toward 0 when squared” and “Values in [8, 12] shrink toward 0 when standardized & squared” sat on the crest. Both now sit in the headroom above it, including when the panel is short and the bell is at its tallest. A narrow slot wraps the sentence so it stays inside the plot. The wording, the cases, and the citation version are unchanged.

- Pearson’s χ² goodness-of-fit no longer scrolls inside a panel. The decomposition table scrolled sideways (a `.table-scroll` wrapper), and the live sum scrolled on its own once six terms were written out. Each category is now a labeled grid in the card: four columns on a wide card, two on a narrow one, with the contribution on the same line as its label. The sum breaks after each term. Category names on the wheel wrap instead of being cut off, and inside the half-width card the probability slider sits on its own row. The page is still the only vertical scroller. Wheels, presets, spins, residuals, the χ² arithmetic, the density, Cochran’s rule, and the Monte Carlo overlay are unchanged. The citation version is unchanged.

- Variance Explained uses a shorter page. The mode switch sits with the title, the slider and the distance toggles share one card, and the scatter plot and the variance breakdown sit side by side with less padding. The scatter keeps its size from that card. The model, the formulae, and the light theme are unchanged. Phone and coarse-pointer range targets stay 44px.

- Multiple Regression and ANOVA Equivalence keeps the stage controls next to the plot. The inspector opens on Controls (interaction, slice, morph, and the stage switches). Fitted OLS model, true population parameters, and variance partitioning are on Model / Fit, so those cards no longer sit above the sliders. Show description / Hide description is the same control as before, on a wide screen as well as a phone, and it still uses `demo-library-regression-anova-step-description`. With no saved choice, the step text starts closed when the window is at most 960px wide or at most 900px tall, and open on a taller wide screen. A saved choice is kept everywhere. A stacked layout (that 960px breakpoint, other than a short landscape phone) scrolls the page only. The plot is `min(52vh, 90vw)`, and the stage card and the inspector sit in that scroll, with no inner vertical scrollbar. Formulae wrap. A short landscape phone keeps the full plot under the compact header. The cite strip stays in the page, just past that plot, so it is not pinned over the legend. Inspector on that plot opens the same controls; Plot returns to the figure. On a narrow screen the stage and camera bars scroll sideways, so the plot and the controls are not pushed down by a stack of pills. The stage card no longer has its own scrollbar. The model, the scene, and the shared 44px range target are unchanged.

- Multiple Regression and ANOVA Equivalence on a landscape phone. The plot had been capped at 240px under the header, and the stage card plus a two-row legend left about a hundred pixels of the surface. The plot now fills the screen under the header, and the stage and camera bars scroll sideways. The legend stays on the plot and wraps, because a scrollbar over the canvas painted a black band across the scene. A Show description control on the plot and in the sidebar opens the step text again. The choice is kept in this browser. Stage, camera, action, and radio controls, and that description control, are 44px on a phone or a coarse pointer. A wide screen with a fine pointer keeps the compact controls. The canvas still follows its box.

- Fullscreen on Catalog and Guided Tracks still calls the browser Fullscreen API on the demo pane. The pane’s scroll height is measured while the embedded page is the pane’s own height. A demonstration that sizes itself from the viewport (100vh, 100%, or a canvas that follows its wrapper) no longer makes that iframe grow without bound. Covariance and Correlation was already stable. The Noematachograph, the probability distributions map, and the chi-square distribution were not: each kept reporting a taller page after the iframe grew, and the figure ran off without a limit. The enter control stays hidden under 480px.

- Guided Track connection notes and Key points typeset subscripts and superscripts. Pearson's r, the pooled and difference-score standard deviations, ANOVA mean squares and sums of squares, chi-square, and the Poisson count no longer show a raw underscore or `^{}`. The same notation on the paired-t figure labels is set as math text. The zero-spread note on Covariance and Correlation, and the residual-plot axis on Ordinary Least Squares, use the same subscript treatment. Meaning of each line is unchanged.

- Chi-Square Distribution has a second case, observed and expected counts, beside the sum of squared standard normals. A fair coin (heads and tails, each expecting half the tosses) or a fair die (faces 1–6, each expecting one sixth of the rolls) draws a sample of counts. The default sample is 60 trials, so the die expects 10 on a face and the coin expects 30 on a side. The sum Σ (O − E)² / E is marked on the same χ² curve. Degrees of freedom are one less than the number of categories: 1 for the coin, 5 for the die. Repeated draws fill the histogram. ## [0.1.8] - 2026-11-15

### Added

- From Binomial Counts to Chi-Square, under Statistics → Sampling distributions and intervals, after Chi-Square as Squared Distance and before Pearson’s χ² Goodness-of-Fit. A coin (k = 2) or a die (k = 6) draws binomial or multinomial counts. The focused category is a marginal binomial: expected count n·pᵢ, raw error, and a normal approximation from the central limit theorem. Standardizing that error gives Zᵢ. Zᵢ² divides by Eᵢ(1 − pᵢ); each Pearson cell (Oᵢ − Eᵢ)² / Eᵢ divides by Eᵢ alone. Summing the cells yields χ² with k − 1 degrees of freedom. When k = 2, Z² equals χ². Cochran’s rule flags a minimum expected count below 5. Chi-Square Distribution, Chi-Square as Squared Distance, and Pearson’s χ² Goodness-of-Fit are unchanged. No Guided Track step.

## [0.1.7] - 2026-11-15

### Added

- Chi-Square as Squared Distance, under Statistics → Sampling distributions and intervals, after Chi-Square Distribution and before Pearson’s χ² Goodness-of-Fit. A Gaussian dart lands on a 1D rail, a 2D board, or in a 3D chamber. Squared distance from the bullseye is a generalized chi-square when the axis spreads differ, a scaled chi-square when they are equal, and a standard chi-square when each axis is divided by its spread. The page opens on the 2D generalized case. Each dart keeps its latent standard normals, so degrees of freedom, spreads, and normalization stay consistent. Throws are capped at 2,000. The histogram uses the exact density, including the two-axis Bessel form and the singularity-safe convolution for three unequal axes. Chi-Square Distribution and Pearson’s χ² Goodness-of-Fit are unchanged. No Guided Track step.
