Cite

Cite the library as a whole when a syllabus, paper, or slide deck refers to the collection. The version identifies this release.

APA 7

Ko, Y. H. (2026). Teaching materials library (Version 0.1.3). https://yiuhong.github.io/demo-library/

The title is in APA sentence case.

BibTeX

@misc{ko2026teachingmaterials,
  author  = {Ko, Yiu Hong},
  title   = {{Teaching Materials Library}},
  year    = {2026},
  version = {0.1.3},
  url     = {https://yiuhong.github.io/demo-library/},
  note    = {Version 0.1.3, last updated 29 October 2026}
}

Citing one demo

Keep the library citation above when you mean the whole collection. For a single demo, name that demo, then the library, the version, and the URL of the demo page. Titles come from the catalog.

Central Limit Theorem (CLT). Teaching Materials Library (Version 0.1.3). https://yiuhong.github.io/demo-library/statistics/central_limit_theorem.html

This example is the Central Limit Theorem (CLT) demo. On a demo page, Cite this demo in the license strip copies the same pattern for that page.

Reuse

What you may do

  • Assign the URL of the library, or of one demo, in a course, a syllabus, or a learning platform.
  • Fork the repository, adapt a demo for your own students, and host that copy.
  • Put a screenshot or a still on a slide or a handout, and keep the credit line with it.
  • Embed a demo in an iframe. These pages are ordinary HTML and can be framed. Give the frame a tall height; some demos are laid out for a full window.

What is not OK

  • Do not present a copy as the official Teaching Materials Library, and do not imply that Yiu Hong Ko endorses it.
  • Do not remove the attribution. Keep the author's name, a link to the source, and the CC BY 4.0 notice. If you changed the material, start the credit line with "Adapted from".
  • Do not add legal terms or technical locks that stop the next person from reusing the material under the same license.

Accessibility

The library aims for usable keyboard access and readable contrast. That is a goal for these pages, not a finished audit, and this site does not state a conformance level. If a control cannot be reached from the keyboard, or text is hard to read, report it on GitHub Issues and name the page.

Report a mistake
