Why $n-1$? The Minimization Property of the Mean

Mathematically, the sample mean is the exact point that minimizes the sum of squared deviations. If you calculate the sum of squares around any other number (including the true population mean), the result will form a U-shaped parabola and will be larger. Because we don't know the population mean, we are forced to use the sample mean, which artificially shrinks our variance estimate. Dividing by $n-1$ corrects for this artificial shrinkage!
