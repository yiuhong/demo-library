Dividing the raw error $O_i - E_i$ by the binomial standard deviation $\sqrt{n p_i(1-p_i)}$ gives a standard normal $Z_i$. $Z_i^2$ divides by $E_i(1-p_i)$. Each Pearson cell $(O_i - E_i)^2 / E_i$ divides by $E_i$ alone, because stage 3 adds every category.
