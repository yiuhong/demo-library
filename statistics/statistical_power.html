While H₁ is usually "μ ≠ μ₀", power calculation requires a specific assumed true mean.

Cohen's d (Effect Size) Small (d = 0.40)
d = |μ₁ - μ₀| / σ = |2.0 - 0.0| / 5.0 = 0.40

What it means: Cohen's d measures the distance between μ₀ and μ₁ in units of standard deviation (σ). It represents the true strength of the effect in the population, independently of sample size (n).

Small: d ≈ 0.2 Medium: d ≈ 0.5 Large: d ≈ 0.8
