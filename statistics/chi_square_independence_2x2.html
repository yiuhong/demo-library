2×2 χ² test of independence

Under independence, each expected count is the row total times the column total, divided by N. With those margins fixed, the four residuals share one magnitude, and only one cell is free to move.

Edit a count, or slide the residual while the margins are fixed. The mosaic and the χ² sum update together. Keys 1, 2, and 3 change how much of the calculation is on screen.

Reference. The χ² criterion is Pearson (1900), On the criterion that a given system of deviations from the probable in the case of a correlated system of variables is such that it can be reasonably supposed to have arisen from random sampling (Philosophical Magazine, Series 5, 50, 157–175). The expected-count check is Cochran (1954), Some methods for strengthening the common χ² tests (Biometrics, 10, 417–451). The continuity correction is Yates (1934), Contingency tables involving small numbers and the χ² test (Supplement to the Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, 1, 217–235).
