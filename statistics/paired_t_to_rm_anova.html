C. Mathematical Equivalence
Identities Linking Paired $t$ & RM-ANOVA
Parallel paths = tiny Residual: High $r \implies$ large $SS_{\text{Subjects}} \implies$ uniform slopes (small $s_D^2$) $\implies$ tiny $SS_{\text{Residual}}$.
Identity 1: $t^2 = F_{(1, N-1)}$ (for $k=2$)
Identity 2: $SS_{\text{Subjects}} = \frac{(N-1)(s_1^2 + s_2^2 + 2rs_1s_2)}{2}$
Identity 3: $SS_{\text{Residual}} = \frac{(N-1)s_D^2}{2}$
Identity 4: $SS_{\text{Time}} = \frac{N \cdot \bar{D}^2}{2}$
Omnibus Test Active
The strict mathematical equivalence ($t^2 = F$) applies exclusively to $k=2$ designs.
