Notice the math: Both Sum of Squares (SS) and Degrees of Freedom (df) add up perfectly. However, the unbiased sample Variance, or Mean Square (MS), is calculated by dividing SS by df. Because the denominators are different for each component, Mean Squares do NOT add up (Total ≠ Explained + Unexplained).
