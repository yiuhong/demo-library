What is this?

This is a network analysis visualization based on data from over 1.25 million respondents. Instead of grouping strengths under hidden theoretical categories, it maps the direct, complex relationships between character strengths.

The lines (edges) represent correlations between strengths (nodes) after controlling for all other influences in the network. For example, a thick blue line between Curiosity and Love of Learning means they are strongly, positively linked in people's real-world survey responses.

What Does This Tell Us?

  • Communities: Instead of perfectly matching the original 6 theoretical virtues, the Spinglass algorithm found the data naturally clustered into 4 distinct communities: Discernment, Responsibility, Interpersonal, and Energy.
  • Gratitude's Influence: Gratitude has the highest Expected Influence. This means it has the highest sum of all connections to other strengths, acting as the major hub in the network.
  • Zest as the Ultimate Bridge: Zest has the highest Bridge Centrality. This means it acts as the primary connector linking entirely different communities of traits together.
  • Natural Trade-offs: The red lines reveal that not all positive traits harmonize. For instance, high Prudence negatively correlates with Perseverance, Forgiveness, and Creativity, showing complex trade-offs in character.
How was this network created?
  • GGM & LASSO: Creates a network of reliable connections by filtering out weak, likely false correlations.
  • Community Analysis (Spinglass): An algorithm that automatically finds groups of strengths that naturally cluster together.
  • Network Stability: Confirmed the map is reliable by repeatedly testing smaller random groups of the 1.25M respondents.
Interaction Guide
  • Hover over a node to highlight its connections.
  • Scroll to zoom in and out.
  • Blue lines: Positive correlations (traits that increase together).
  • Red lines: Negative correlations (traits that act as trade-offs).
  • Line thickness: Indicates the strength of the correlation (thicker = stronger).
