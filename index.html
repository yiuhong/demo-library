Features

  • Coverage

    The demonstrations cover statistics.

  • Interactive figures

    The figures are interactive and intuitive. Change a control and the figure updates.

  • Teach and Learn

    Learn, the default, is the full detailed view. Teach is a quieter class view: the figure, the live equation, and the controls stay, and the longer notes are hidden.
