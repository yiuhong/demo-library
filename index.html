Features

  • Coverage

    The demonstrations cover content from introductory to advanced topics in statistics. Designed for everyone — from absolute beginners and high school students to undergraduates, postgraduates, and researchers.

  • Interactive figures

    The figures are interactive and intuitive. Change a control and the figure updates.

  • Teach and Learn

    Learn is the full detailed view, including self-tests with collectible completion cards. Teach is a quieter class view: the figure, the live equation, and the controls stay, and the longer notes are hidden.

  • References

    When a demonstration's teaching depends on a textbook, a classic paper, or another reputable source, a short teaching note names that source.

  • Open source

    Project Viz is open source. Teaching text, explanations, and figures are CC BY 4.0, and the source code is MIT. See Cite & reuse.
