AI in Therapy: Transcript Analysis 🛋️

This tool demonstrates Natural Language Processing (NLP).

Keyword Tagging vs. NLP: Simple keyword taggers blindly look for individual words. If a patient says "I am not angry", a keyword tagger just sees "angry" and flags it, leading to incorrect assessments. True NLP understands context and grammar, grouping modifiers together (like "not angry" or "didn't panic") to capture the actual meaning as a single phrase.
